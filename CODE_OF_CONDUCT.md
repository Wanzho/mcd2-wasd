# Code of conduct

wasdmod is a small hobby project. This page says what to expect from each other in its issues, pull requests and discussions.

## What's expected

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are players, not programmers, and English is often a second language.
- Talk about the mod, the bug or the code, and leave the person out of it.
- Disagree by explaining your reasons. "This broke for me, here is the log" helps more than "this is bad".
- Accept a "no" on a feature request. The mod stays small on purpose.

## What isn't allowed

- Insults, harassment, slurs or sexual content.
- Posting someone's private information.
- Spam and advertising that has nothing to do with the mod.
- Cheats, or requests for help cheating in online play.

## Reporting

If someone breaks these rules, use GitHub's **Report content** link on the comment or issue. If it needs the maintainer's attention, you can also open an issue that says so without repeating the offending text.

The maintainer may edit or delete comments, close or lock threads, and block accounts that keep breaking these rules.

## Scope

This applies to the [mcd2-wasd repository](https://github.com/Wanzho/mcd2-wasd) and to the mod's pages on other sites when someone is acting on the project's behalf.
