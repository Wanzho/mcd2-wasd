; Author's keybinds for the Minecraft Dungeons II controller mod.
; Bow on both side buttons; inventory (E, I), quests (V, J), social (/) and
; teleport (X) on the controller so you can keep moving; Tab opens the map.
; Only the keys you press change; the game's own keys stay at their defaults.
; Put this file in the game's Win64 folder (Windows setup: Load layout file) and
; restart the game. The newest of default.txt, author.txt and wasdmod*.txt is used.
